kooba-helper bookmarklet

Drag the button below to your bookmarks bar (or right-click → "Bookmark this link"). When you are on an Abook audiobook listing, click it to add search links for NZBIndex, BinSearch, NZBKing and an NZBDonkey highlight block plus a “Copy Title” button.

It works with Chromium, Brave and Firefox (and can be used as a normal bookmarklet — click it on every page you want to use it on).
